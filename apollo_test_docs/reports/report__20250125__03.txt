Title: Investment Performance Review Q4 2024
Date: 2025-01-25
Category: report
Source: Westfield Asset Management

PERFORMANCE SUMMARY

The Westfield Growth Fund delivered a total return of 8.2% for the fourth quarter and 24.6% for the full year 2024. These results outperformed the benchmark S&P 500 index returns of 7.1% and 23.3% respectively.

ATTRIBUTION ANALYSIS

Sector allocation contributed 1.8% of excess return, with overweight positions in technology and healthcare providing the largest positive impact. Security selection added 0.9% of alpha, primarily from strong performance in semiconductor and biotechnology holdings.

TOP CONTRIBUTORS

The fund's largest positive contributors were positions in Nvidia Corporation (+42%), Eli Lilly (+38%), and Microsoft (+29%). These three positions collectively added 4.2 percentage points to quarterly performance.

TOP DETRACTORS

Underperformance came from holdings in Pfizer (-18%), PayPal (-12%), and 3M Company (-8%). The pharmaceutical sector faced headwinds from patent expirations and pricing pressures.

PORTFOLIO POSITIONING

The fund ended the quarter with 92% equity exposure and 8% cash reserves. Average market capitalization of holdings is $185 billion. The portfolio price-to-earnings ratio of 28.5x compares to the benchmark at 24.2x, reflecting the growth orientation.
