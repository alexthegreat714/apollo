Title: Municipal Bond Market Analysis - January 2025
Date: 2025-02-02
Category: report
Source: Capital Markets Research Group

MARKET OVERVIEW

The municipal bond market experienced increased volatility in January 2025 as investors reassessed interest rate expectations. The Bloomberg Municipal Bond Index returned -0.8% for the month after a strong finish to 2024.

SUPPLY AND DEMAND DYNAMICS

New issuance totaled $28 billion in January, down 15% from the prior year period. Refunding volume declined sharply as higher rates made refinancing uneconomical for most issuers. Mutual fund flows turned negative with $2.1 billion in outflows during the month.

CREDIT DEVELOPMENTS

State and local government finances remain healthy overall. Tax revenue growth moderated to 3% year-over-year from 5% in mid-2024. Pension funding levels improved slightly, with the average funded ratio reaching 78% from 75% a year ago.

YIELD CURVE ANALYSIS

The AAA municipal yield curve flattened during the month. The 10-year AAA yield rose 18 basis points to 3.42% while the 30-year increased only 8 basis points to 4.15%. The ratio of municipal to Treasury yields fell to 82% from 85%, indicating relative value concerns.

SECTOR OUTLOOK

Healthcare revenue bonds face ongoing pressure from reimbursement uncertainty. Transportation credits benefit from strong traffic volumes. Higher education bonds show mixed performance depending on enrollment trends and endowment returns.
