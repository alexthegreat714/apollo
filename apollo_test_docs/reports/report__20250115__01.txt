Title: Q4 2024 Earnings Report - Meridian Technologies Inc.
Date: 2025-01-15
Category: report
Source: Meridian Technologies Investor Relations

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Meridian Technologies Inc. reported strong fourth quarter results with revenue of $3.8 billion, representing a 12% increase year-over-year. Net income reached $645 million, or $2.85 per diluted share, exceeding analyst expectations of $2.62 per share.

OPERATIONAL HIGHLIGHTS

The cloud services division continued to drive growth, with segment revenue increasing 28% to $1.4 billion. Enterprise software licenses grew 8% while consulting services remained flat. International markets contributed 42% of total revenue, up from 38% in the prior year quarter.

FINANCIAL POSITION

Cash and equivalents totaled $8.2 billion at quarter end. The company reduced long-term debt by $500 million during the quarter, bringing total debt to $4.1 billion. Operating cash flow was $1.1 billion, and free cash flow reached $780 million after capital expenditures of $320 million.

FORWARD GUIDANCE

Management raised full-year 2025 guidance to $15.8-16.2 billion in revenue and $11.50-12.00 in earnings per share. The company expects continued strength in cloud services and is investing $2 billion in AI infrastructure over the next 18 months.
