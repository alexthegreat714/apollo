Title: Annual Credit Risk Assessment Report 2024
Date: 2025-01-20
Category: report
Source: First National Bank Risk Management Division

OVERVIEW

This report summarizes the credit risk profile of First National Bank's loan portfolio as of December 31, 2024. Total loan exposure stands at $42.8 billion across commercial, residential, and consumer segments.

PORTFOLIO COMPOSITION

Commercial real estate loans represent 35% of the portfolio at $15.0 billion, followed by residential mortgages at 28% ($12.0 billion), commercial and industrial loans at 22% ($9.4 billion), and consumer loans at 15% ($6.4 billion).

CREDIT QUALITY METRICS

Non-performing loans decreased to 1.2% of total loans from 1.5% in the prior year. The allowance for credit losses stands at $520 million, representing 1.22% of total loans. Net charge-offs for the year totaled $185 million, down from $210 million in 2023.

CONCENTRATION RISK

Geographic concentration remains within policy limits, with no single state exceeding 18% of the portfolio. Industry concentration in commercial loans shows elevated exposure to healthcare at 14% and technology at 12%, both within approved thresholds.

RECOMMENDATIONS

Management recommends maintaining current reserve levels given economic uncertainty. Enhanced monitoring is advised for commercial real estate office properties in metropolitan markets where vacancy rates exceed 15%.
