Title: Major Banks Report Record Profits on Higher Interest Income
Date: 2025-02-15
Category: news
Source: Wall Street Journal

JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, and Wells Fargo reported combined fourth-quarter profits of $28 billion, benefiting from elevated interest rates that boosted lending margins. Net interest income across the three banks rose 15% year-over-year to $58 billion.

JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon cautioned that net interest income would moderate in 2025 as deposit costs continue rising and loan growth remains subdued. The bank projected NII of $88 billion for the year, down from $92 billion in 2024.

Credit quality remained strong overall, though provisions for loan losses increased 8% as banks prepared for potential weakening in commercial real estate and credit card portfolios. Charge-off rates on consumer loans edged higher but stayed below historical averages.

Investment banking revenues recovered from 2024 lows, with advisory fees up 20% and equity underwriting surging on strong IPO activity. Trading revenues benefited from volatility in rates and currencies, with fixed income trading up 12%.

Bank stocks rallied on the results, with the KBW Bank Index gaining 4% for the week. Analysts raised price targets citing better-than-expected margins and manageable credit deterioration.
