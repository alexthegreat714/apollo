Title: Tech Giants Lead Market Rally on AI Infrastructure Spending
Date: 2025-02-12
Category: news
Source: Bloomberg

Nvidia, Microsoft, and Amazon drove major indices to new highs as investors bet on continued growth in artificial intelligence infrastructure. The Nasdaq Composite rose 2.3% to close above 18,000 for the first time.

Nvidia shares surged 8% after the company announced $100 billion in data center chip orders for 2025, exceeding analyst expectations. CEO Jensen Huang highlighted enterprise adoption of AI for applications ranging from customer service to drug discovery.

Microsoft reported that Azure cloud revenue grew 29% year-over-year, with AI services contributing six percentage points of growth. The company's Copilot AI assistant now has 1.5 million enterprise subscribers, up from 180,000 a year ago.

Amazon Web Services saw AI workloads drive accelerating growth, with executives calling out demand for training and inference compute from both startups and established enterprises. Capital expenditure guidance of $80 billion for 2025 underscores the scale of AI investment.

Some analysts expressed concern about the concentration of market gains in a handful of large-cap tech stocks. The top seven companies by market capitalization now represent 32% of the S&P 500, the highest concentration since the dot-com era.
