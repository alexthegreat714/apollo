Title: Housing Market Shows Signs of Stabilization as Mortgage Rates Ease
Date: 2025-02-05
Category: news
Source: CNBC

The US housing market is showing tentative signs of recovery as mortgage rates decline from 2024 peaks, spurring a modest increase in buyer activity. The 30-year fixed mortgage rate fell to 6.5%, down from 7.2% in October.

January existing home sales rose 3.1% month-over-month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 4.2 million, the first increase in six months. New home sales also improved as builders offered incentives including rate buydowns and price reductions.

Inventory remains historically tight at 3.1 months of supply, well below the 5-6 months considered balanced. Many homeowners with sub-4% mortgages continue to hold their properties, constraining the available stock of existing homes for sale.

Home prices increased 4.5% year-over-year nationally, with Sunbelt markets showing moderation while coastal cities maintained stronger appreciation. The median existing home price reached $385,000 in January.

Homebuilder stocks rallied on the improving outlook, with the SPDR S&P Homebuilders ETF up 8% year-to-date. DR Horton and Lennar raised 2025 guidance citing better-than-expected order trends in the spring selling season.
