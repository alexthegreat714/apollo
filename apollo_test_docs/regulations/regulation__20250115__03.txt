Title: Basel III Endgame - Capital Requirements for Large Banks
Date: 2025-01-15
Category: regulation
Source: Federal Reserve Board

OVERVIEW

The Basel III endgame rules establish new risk-based capital requirements for large bank holding companies with $100 billion or more in total assets. The rules expand the use of standardized approaches for credit risk and operational risk, reducing reliance on internal models.

CREDIT RISK STANDARDIZED APPROACH

The standardized approach for credit risk assigns risk weights based on exposure characteristics. Residential mortgages receive risk weights from 40% to 90% based on loan-to-value ratios. Corporate exposures are risk-weighted at 65% to 150% depending on external ratings where available.

OPERATIONAL RISK

The standardized approach for operational risk replaces internal models with a formulaic calculation based on historical net interest income, fee income, and operating expenses. Banks with higher historical losses face additional capital requirements through an internal loss multiplier.

MARKET RISK

The Fundamental Review of the Trading Book introduces the standardized approach and internal models approach for market risk capital. The standardized approach uses sensitivity-based measures for delta, vega, and curvature risks across prescribed risk factors.

IMPLEMENTATION TIMELINE

The final rules are expected to take effect July 1, 2025, with a three-year transition period ending July 1, 2028. During the transition, firms will report under both current and revised standards, with the binding constraint being the higher of the two.

OUTPUT FLOOR

An output floor ensures that risk-weighted assets calculated under internal models cannot fall below 72.5% of standardized approach calculations, phased in over the transition period.
