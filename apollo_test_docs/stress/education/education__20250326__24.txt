Title: Credit Scores and Lending Criteria
Date: 2025-03-26
Category: education
Source: Apollo Synthetic Finance Set
Priority: 4

Credit scores are numerical representations of creditworthiness derived from credit report information. Lenders use these scores to assess the probability of loan repayment and determine interest rates and credit limits.

FICO Score ranges (most widely used):

Exceptional: 800-850
Very Good: 740-799
Good: 670-739
Fair: 580-669
Poor: 300-579

FICO Score components:

Payment History: 35%
- On-time payments, delinquencies, collections

Amounts Owed: 30%
- Credit utilization ratio, total balances

Length of Credit History: 15%
- Age of oldest account, average account age

Credit Mix: 10%
- Variety of credit types (cards, loans, mortgage)

New Credit: 10%
- Recent inquiries, newly opened accounts

Numerical Example:

Credit Utilization Calculation:
Total credit limits: $20,000
Total balances: $4,000

Utilization Rate = $4,000 / $20,000 = 20%

A utilization rate below 30% is generally recommended, with below 10% considered optimal.

Impact on interest rates:

Mortgage example for $300,000 loan, 30-year fixed:
- Credit score 760+: 6.5% APR, monthly payment $1,896
- Credit score 680: 7.0% APR, monthly payment $1,996
- Credit score 620: 8.0% APR, monthly payment $2,201

Total interest paid over 30 years:
- 760+ score: $382,560
- 680 score: $418,560
- 620 score: $492,360

The difference between highest and lowest scores: $109,800 in additional interest.

Lending criteria beyond credit score:

1. Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)
   DTI = Monthly Debt Payments / Gross Monthly Income
   Most lenders require DTI below 43%

2. Loan-to-Value Ratio (LTV)
   LTV = Loan Amount / Property Value
   Higher LTV may require mortgage insurance

3. Employment and income verification
4. Collateral value for secured loans
5. Down payment or equity contribution

Credit scores update monthly as creditors report to bureaus. Payment history impact diminishes over time, with most negative items falling off after seven years.
