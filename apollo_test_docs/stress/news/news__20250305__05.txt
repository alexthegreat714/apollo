Title: China Announces Fiscal Stimulus Package to Boost Growth
Date: 2025-03-05
Category: news
Source: Financial Times

China unveiled a 3 trillion yuan ($410 billion) fiscal stimulus package at the National People's Congress, targeting infrastructure investment, consumer subsidies, and support for the struggling property sector. The measures aim to achieve the government's 5% GDP growth target for 2025.

INFRASTRUCTURE SPENDING

The package allocates 1.2 trillion yuan for infrastructure projects including high-speed rail, renewable energy, and digital infrastructure. Local governments will receive special bond quotas of 4.5 trillion yuan, up from 3.9 trillion in 2024. The central government deficit is set at 4% of GDP.

PROPERTY MEASURES

Authorities will establish a 300 billion yuan fund to purchase unsold homes for affordable housing. Mortgage down payment requirements are reduced to 15% for first-time buyers nationwide. Cities with excess inventory can remove purchase restrictions entirely.

CONSUMER SUBSIDIES

The government will expand trade-in subsidies for automobiles and home appliances, allocating 200 billion yuan through 2025. Individual income tax deductions for childcare and eldercare expenses increase significantly.

MARKET REACTION

Chinese equities rallied with the CSI 300 gaining 2.8%. Property developer bonds rose sharply on the housing support measures. Analysts noted the package addresses demand-side weakness but structural issues in the property sector require longer-term solutions.
