Title: OPEC+ Maintains Production Cuts Amid Price Volatility
Date: 2025-03-12
Category: news
Source: Reuters

OPEC+ ministers agreed to extend current production cuts of 2.2 million barrels per day through the second quarter, seeking to support oil prices amid concerns about global demand growth. Brent crude rose 2.5% to $82 per barrel following the announcement.

PRODUCTION POLICY

Saudi Arabia will maintain its voluntary 1 million barrel per day cut through June. Russia confirmed compliance with its commitment to reduce output by 500,000 barrels per day. The group will reassess market conditions at its June meeting before determining policy for the second half.

DEMAND CONCERNS

The decision reflects uncertainty about oil demand from China, which grew just 1.8% year-over-year in February. IEA projects global demand growth of 1.0 million barrels per day in 2025, below OPEC's forecast of 1.8 million. Non-OPEC supply growth from the US, Brazil, and Guyana continues to add to market supply.

US SHALE RESPONSE

American producers remain disciplined despite improving breakeven costs. Rig counts have stabilized around 580, down from 620 a year ago. Private operators continue to outproduce while public companies prioritize shareholder returns.

PRICE OUTLOOK

Analysts see Brent trading in a $75-85 range for 2025 assuming no major supply disruptions. Upside risks include escalation of Middle East conflicts, while downside risks center on weaker-than-expected Chinese economic growth.
