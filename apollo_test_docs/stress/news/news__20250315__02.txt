Title: Major Banks Report Better Than Expected Q1 Earnings
Date: 2025-03-15
Category: news
Source: Bloomberg

JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, and Citigroup exceeded analyst estimates in first-quarter earnings releases, buoyed by stronger-than-expected trading revenue and resilient net interest income. The KBW Bank Index rose 3.2% on the results.

JPMORGAN CHASE

JPMorgan reported earnings per share of $4.85 versus estimates of $4.52. Net interest income reached $23.8 billion, down 3% year-over-year but above guidance. Investment banking fees surged 28% on strong debt underwriting and advisory activity. CEO Jamie Dimon cautioned about elevated geopolitical risks.

BANK OF AMERICA

BofA earned $0.92 per share against consensus of $0.85. Net interest income stabilized at $14.2 billion as deposit cost increases moderated. Wealth management revenue grew 12% from strong client asset flows. Credit card charge-offs rose but remained within guidance.

CREDIT QUALITY

Across the four largest banks, provisions for credit losses increased 8% sequentially to $8.5 billion. Management teams noted normalization in consumer credit rather than systemic deterioration. Commercial real estate reserves remained elevated.

OUTLOOK

Bank executives expressed cautious optimism about 2025, citing potential tailwinds from rate cuts and improving capital markets activity. NII guidance was maintained or slightly raised across the group.
