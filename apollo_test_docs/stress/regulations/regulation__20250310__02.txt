Title: DOL Retirement Security Rule - Implementation Guide
Date: 2025-03-10
Category: regulation
Source: Department of Labor

FIDUCIARY DEFINITION

Under the Retirement Security Rule, a person is a fiduciary if they make investment recommendations to retirement investors for a fee, hold out as providing advice, and address specific securities or investment management decisions. The one-time recommendation exception is eliminated.

COVERED RETIREMENT INVESTORS

The rule covers participants in ERISA plans, IRA owners, and owners of health savings accounts and education savings accounts. Both individual and institutional retirement investors are covered when receiving investment recommendations.

ROLLOVER RECOMMENDATIONS

Fiduciaries must document the specific reasons why a rollover recommendation is in the investor's best interest. Documentation must compare the retirement investor's existing plan against the recommended IRA, including fees, available investments, and services.

PROHIBITED TRANSACTION EXEMPTIONS

PTE 2020-02 is amended to align with the new fiduciary definition. Financial institutions must acknowledge fiduciary status in writing and cannot use exculpatory clauses. Compensation structures must be reasonable relative to services provided.

ENFORCEMENT CONSIDERATIONS

The Department will prioritize enforcement against recommendations with significant conflicts of interest, high-cost products, and complex fee structures. Class action liability increases exposure for widespread compliance failures.

PRACTICAL STEPS

Firms should update compliance policies, train personnel, revise compensation arrangements that create conflicts, and implement documentation systems for rollover recommendations.
