Title: SEC Private Fund Adviser Rule Summary
Date: 2025-03-05
Category: regulation
Source: Securities and Exchange Commission

QUARTERLY STATEMENTS

Private fund advisers must provide quarterly statements to investors including detailed performance information, fee and expense disclosure, and portfolio investment compensation. Statements must distinguish between fund-level and investment-level fees.

MANDATORY AUDITS

Private funds must obtain annual audits from independent public accountants registered with the PCAOB. Audit reports must be distributed to investors within 120 days of fiscal year end (180 days for fund of funds).

ADVISER-LED SECONDARIES

For adviser-led secondary transactions, advisers must obtain a fairness opinion or valuation opinion from an independent opinion provider. Material business relationships with the adviser must be disclosed.

PREFERENTIAL TREATMENT

Side letter terms cannot provide preferential redemption rights if they would have a material negative effect on other investors. Certain preferential terms must be disclosed to all current and prospective investors.

PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES

The rule prohibits charging fees for unperformed services, borrowing from the fund without investor consent, and reducing clawbacks through taxes. Certain prohibitions are subject to disclosure-based exceptions.

GRANDFATHERING

Existing contractual arrangements are grandfathered for certain provisions including preferential treatment restrictions. However, quarterly statement and audit requirements apply to all funds regardless of formation date.
