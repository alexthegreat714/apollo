Title: FinCEN Anti-Money Laundering Requirements for Investment Advisers
Date: 2025-02-20
Category: regulation
Source: Financial Crimes Enforcement Network

PROPOSED RULE OVERVIEW

FinCEN proposes to include certain investment advisers in the definition of "financial institution" under the Bank Secrecy Act. Covered advisers would be subject to AML program requirements, SAR filing obligations, and certain recordkeeping requirements.

COVERED INVESTMENT ADVISERS

The rule covers SEC-registered investment advisers and exempt reporting advisers. State-registered advisers without SEC reporting obligations are not covered in the initial rule. FinCEN may expand coverage in subsequent rulemaking.

AML Program Components:
-----------------------------------
Element           | Description
Policies/Procedures| Risk-based written AML program
Compliance Officer | Designated qualified individual
Training          | Ongoing employee education
Independent Testing| Periodic program assessment
CDD/CIP          | Customer identification & verification
-----------------------------------

SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY REPORTING

Advisers must file SARs for transactions of $5,000 or more involving suspected illegal activity, structuring to avoid reporting, or lacking lawful purpose. Reports are due within 30 calendar days of detection. Continuing activity requires follow-up SARs.

CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE

Advisers must implement risk-based customer due diligence including verifying identity, understanding the nature of client relationships, and conducting ongoing monitoring. Enhanced due diligence applies to high-risk clients including PEPs and high-risk jurisdictions.

BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP

For legal entity clients, advisers must identify and verify beneficial owners holding 25% or more equity and individuals with significant control. Beneficial ownership information must be updated when changes are detected.

IMPLEMENTATION PERIOD

FinCEN proposes a 12-month compliance period from the effective date. Advisers should begin risk assessments, technology evaluations, and policy development in anticipation of final rule publication.
