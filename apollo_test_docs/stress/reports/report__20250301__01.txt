Period_End: 2025-03-31

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The semiconductor industry posted robust growth in Q1 2025, driven by sustained demand for AI accelerators and data center infrastructure. Total industry revenue reached $162 billion, up 18% year-over-year and 4% sequentially. Memory segment showed particular strength with DRAM prices rising 15% quarter-over-quarter.

MARKET SEGMENTATION ANALYSIS

Data center and AI chips accounted for 42% of total revenue, up from 35% in Q1 2024. Automotive semiconductors grew 12% as EV adoption accelerated. Consumer electronics stabilized after two years of inventory correction, with smartphone chip demand returning to pre-pandemic levels.

SUPPLY CHAIN DYNAMICS

Leading-edge capacity utilization reached 95% at TSMC and Samsung. Mature node capacity remains constrained for automotive and industrial applications. Capital expenditure across the industry totaled $45 billion in Q1, with 60% allocated to advanced nodes (5nm and below).

OUTLOOK

We maintain our positive outlook for H2 2025, projecting full-year industry revenue of $680 billion (+15% YoY). Key risks include geopolitical tensions affecting trade flows and potential overinvestment in AI capacity.
