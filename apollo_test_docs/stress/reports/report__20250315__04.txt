Title: Banking Sector Credit Quality Review Q1 2025
Date: 2025-03-15
Category: report
Source: Moody's Investors Service
Period_Start: 2025-01-01
Period_End: 2025-03-31

SUMMARY ASSESSMENT

US bank credit quality remains stable but with pockets of emerging stress in commercial real estate and credit cards. Capital ratios are strong, providing cushion against potential losses. We maintain a stable outlook on the sector with negative bias for banks with concentrated CRE exposure.

ASSET QUALITY METRICS

Non-performing loans increased to 0.85% of total loans, up from 0.72% a year ago. Charge-offs rose to 0.65% annualized from 0.48%, primarily driven by credit cards and CRE. Early-stage delinquencies (30-89 days) ticked higher, warranting close monitoring.

CAPITAL AND LIQUIDITY

Common equity tier 1 ratios averaged 12.8% for large banks, well above regulatory minimums. Liquidity coverage ratios remained strong at 118% on average. Deposit flows stabilized following 2024 volatility, though mix shift toward higher-cost funding continues.

COMMERCIAL REAL ESTATE EXPOSURE

Office CRE loans represent the primary area of concern. Banks with office exposure exceeding 200% of capital face elevated risk of rating pressure. We estimate $45 billion in office loans will mature in 2025 requiring refinancing at higher rates.

OUTLOOK

Credit normalization will continue through 2025 but systemic stress is unlikely given strong capital positions. Regional banks with concentrated CRE portfolios face the greatest challenges.
