Title: ECB Governing Council Monetary Policy Decision
Date: 2025-03-15
Category: statement
Source: European Central Bank

The Governing Council today decided to lower the three key ECB interest rates by 25 basis points. The deposit facility rate will be reduced to 3.00 percent, the main refinancing operations rate to 3.15 percent, and the marginal lending facility rate to 3.40 percent, with effect from 19 March 2025.

INFLATION ASSESSMENT

Inflation has continued to decline broadly in line with projections. Headline inflation fell to 2.2 percent in February from 2.4 percent in January. Services inflation remains elevated at 3.8 percent but is expected to moderate as wage growth normalizes. The Governing Council's updated projections see headline inflation averaging 2.3 percent in 2025 and 2.0 percent in 2026.

ECONOMIC OUTLOOK

The euro area economy has remained weak, with GDP growth of 0.1 percent in Q4 2024. Manufacturing continues to contract while services show modest expansion. The labor market remains resilient with unemployment at 6.4 percent. Credit growth to firms and households is gradually recovering.

ECB Staff Projections:
-----------------------------------
           | 2025   | 2026   | 2027
GDP Growth | 0.9%   | 1.5%   | 1.4%
Inflation  | 2.3%   | 2.0%   | 1.9%
-----------------------------------

FORWARD GUIDANCE

The Governing Council will continue to follow a data-dependent and meeting-by-meeting approach to determining the appropriate level and duration of restriction. The Governing Council is determined to ensure that inflation returns to its 2 percent medium-term target in a timely manner.
