Title: Federal Reserve Monetary Policy Statement - February 2025
Date: 2025-02-28
Category: statement
Source: Federal Open Market Committee

The Federal Open Market Committee decided at its February meeting to maintain the target range for the federal funds rate at 4.75 to 5.00 percent. The Committee continues to assess incoming data, the evolving outlook, and the balance of risks.

ECONOMIC CONDITIONS

Recent indicators suggest that economic activity has been expanding at a solid pace. Job gains have remained robust, and the unemployment rate has stabilized at a low level. Inflation has eased over the past year but remains somewhat elevated. The Committee judges that the risks to achieving its employment and inflation goals continue to move into better balance.

POLICY STANCE

The Committee seeks to achieve maximum employment and inflation at the rate of 2 percent over the longer run. In support of these goals, the Committee decided to maintain the federal funds rate target range. In considering any adjustments to the target range, the Committee will carefully assess incoming data, the evolving outlook, and the balance of risks.

BALANCE SHEET

The Committee will continue reducing its holdings of Treasury securities and agency debt and agency mortgage-backed securities, as described in its previously announced plans. The Committee is strongly committed to returning inflation to its 2 percent objective.

Voting for this action: Jerome H. Powell, Chair; John C. Williams, Vice Chair; Michael S. Barr; Michelle W. Bowman; Lisa D. Cook; Mary C. Daly; Philip N. Jefferson; Adriana D. Kugler; Christopher J. Waller.
