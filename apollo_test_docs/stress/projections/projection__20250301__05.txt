Title: Private Equity Performance Projections 2025-2027
Date: 2025-03-01
Category: projection
Source: Cambridge Associates

RETURN EXPECTATIONS

We project private equity net returns of 12-14% annually for 2025-2027 vintage funds, below the 17% realized for 2010-2019 vintages. Higher entry multiples, elevated financing costs, and competitive markets compress returns. Dispersion between top and bottom quartile managers increases.

EXIT ENVIRONMENT

Exit activity should recover from 2024 lows as the IPO market reopens and strategic M&A accelerates. Sponsor-to-sponsor sales remain challenged by valuation gaps. Hold periods continue extending, averaging 6.5 years versus 4.5 years a decade ago.

VALUE CREATION LEVERS

Operational improvement becomes the primary value creation lever as financial engineering contributes less with higher rates. Buy-and-build strategies remain attractive but integration risk increases. Technology enablement drives margin expansion across portfolio companies.

FUNDRAISING OUTLOOK

LP commitments normalize from peak 2021-2022 levels but remain healthy. LPs concentrate allocations with proven managers, disadvantaging emerging funds. Continuation funds provide liquidity solutions for extended holds.

SECTOR PREFERENCES

Healthcare services and technology remain favored sectors with strong growth and multiple expansion potential. Industrials benefit from reshoring tailwinds. Consumer faces headwinds from discretionary spending pressure.
