Title: US Treasury Yield Curve Forecast Q2-Q4 2025
Date: 2025-03-20
Category: projection
Source: Morgan Stanley Fixed Income Research

BASELINE SCENARIO

We project the Fed will begin cutting rates in June 2025, with three 25bp reductions bringing the fed funds rate to 4.00-4.25% by year-end. This should drive a steepening yield curve as short rates decline faster than long rates. The 10-year Treasury yield is expected to trade in a 3.75-4.25% range.

YIELD PROJECTIONS BY TENOR

Short-term yields will follow the fed funds rate lower, while intermediate and long-term yields reflect growth and inflation expectations. We expect the 2s-10s spread to widen from -15bp to +50bp as the curve normalizes.

-----------------------------------

TERM PREMIUM OUTLOOK

Term premium should increase modestly as quantitative tightening continues and Treasury supply remains elevated. We estimate fair value term premium at 50bp for 10-year Treasuries, up from near-zero levels in 2024.

ALTERNATIVE SCENARIOS

Hawkish scenario (25% probability): Inflation reaccelerates, Fed holds rates steady, 10-year yields rise to 4.75%
Dovish scenario (25% probability): Growth slowdown accelerates cuts, 10-year yields fall to 3.25%

INVESTMENT IMPLICATIONS

We recommend extending duration to 6-7 years to capture yield curve normalization. Favor intermediate maturities over long duration given term premium uncertainty. TIPS offer value at current breakevens of 2.3%.
