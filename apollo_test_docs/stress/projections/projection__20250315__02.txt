Title: S&P 500 Earnings Growth Projections 2025-2026
Date: 2025-03-15
Category: projection
Source: JP Morgan Equity Strategy

EARNINGS FORECAST

We project S&P 500 earnings per share of $270 for 2025 (+10% YoY) and $295 for 2026 (+9% YoY). Earnings growth is broadening beyond technology into healthcare, industrials, and financials. Operating margins should expand modestly from efficiency gains and moderating input costs.

SECTOR EARNINGS OUTLOOK

Technology remains the largest earnings contributor, but growth rates are normalizing from exceptional 2024 levels. Healthcare benefits from new drug launches and aging demographics. Financials gain from improved capital markets activity and stable credit.

MARGIN ANALYSIS

Net profit margins are projected at 12.5% for 2025, up from 12.0% in 2024. Labor cost growth is moderating while companies realize benefits from AI-driven productivity improvements. Interest expense stabilizes as companies refinance at lower rates.

VALUATION FRAMEWORK

At current prices, the S&P 500 trades at 20x forward earnings. Our year-end 2025 target of 5,800 implies 21.5x forward earnings on 2026 EPS, justified by the quality of earnings growth and lower rate environment.

RISK FACTORS

Downside risks include margin compression from wage inflation, geopolitical disruptions to supply chains, and AI investment disappointment if revenue monetization lags spending.
