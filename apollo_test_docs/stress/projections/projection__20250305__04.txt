Title: US Housing Market Projections 2025-2026
Date: 2025-03-05
Category: projection
Source: Zillow Economic Research

PRICE FORECAST

We project national home prices to appreciate 3.5% in 2025 and 4.0% in 2026, moderating from the 5.2% gain in 2024. Affordability constraints limit upside while tight inventory provides a floor. Regional divergence continues with Sun Belt markets underperforming coastal areas.

HOME SALES OUTLOOK

Existing home sales are forecast at 4.3 million units in 2025, up 5% from 2024 but still 25% below 2019 levels. Lower mortgage rates unlock some "rate-locked" inventory but sellers remain reluctant to give up sub-4% mortgages. New home sales benefit from builder incentives and available inventory.

AFFORDABILITY ANALYSIS

The median household needs to spend 42% of income on mortgage payments for a median-priced home, well above the historical 30% threshold. Affordability improves as rates decline but remains strained until prices moderate or incomes catch up.

INVENTORY DYNAMICS

Active listings are projected to increase 10% in 2025 as life events force some sales despite rate lock-in. Months of supply should rise from 3.2 to 3.8 months, still below the balanced market level of 5-6 months.

REGIONAL VARIATIONS

Strongest markets: Northeast metros with limited supply and strong employment (Boston, NYC)
Weakest markets: Sun Belt markets with excess pandemic migration and new construction (Austin, Phoenix)
