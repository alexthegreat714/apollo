Title: Fed Rate Cut Timing Scenario Analysis
Date: 2025-03-10
Category: analysis
Source: UBS Macro Strategy

BASELINE SCENARIO - JUNE START

Our baseline anticipates the Fed beginning rate cuts in June 2025, with three 25bp reductions bringing the fed funds rate to 4.00-4.25% by year-end. This scenario assumes inflation continues moderating toward 2.5% by mid-year and labor market cooling proceeds gradually.

EARLY CUT SCENARIO - MARCH START

A March cut becomes possible if financial conditions tighten significantly or inflation undershoots. This would require Core PCE falling to 2.3% and unemployment rising above 4.3%. Market probability currently 10%. This scenario implies 125bp of cuts in 2025.

DELAYED SCENARIO - SEPTEMBER START

If inflation proves sticky above 2.5% through Q2, the Fed delays cuts to September. This scenario assumes shelter inflation moderates slower than expected or services inflation reaccelerates. Market probability 30%. Only two cuts occur in 2025.

NO CUT SCENARIO

A scenario of no cuts in 2025 requires inflation reaccelerating toward 3% or financial conditions easing excessively. This would likely occur from supply shocks (energy, food) or excessive fiscal stimulus. Market probability 15%.

MARKET IMPLICATIONS

The rate path drives asset class performance divergence. Early cuts favor duration and growth stocks. Delayed cuts benefit value and financials. No cuts create risk-off environment with outperformance from quality and low volatility.

POSITIONING RECOMMENDATIONS

Position for baseline with optionality around early scenario. Overweight duration versus consensus, barbell with both early-cycle (rate sensitive) and late-cycle (quality) equity exposures.
