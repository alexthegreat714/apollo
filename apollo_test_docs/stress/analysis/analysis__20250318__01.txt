Title: Nvidia Valuation Analysis - Bull and Bear Cases
Date: 2025-03-18
Category: analysis
Source: Bernstein Equity Research

VALUATION FRAMEWORK

We analyze Nvidia's valuation through three scenarios to capture the range of outcomes from AI infrastructure investment. At current prices of $920, the stock trades at 35x forward P/E and 28x EV/Sales on 2025 estimates. Historical average multiples provide limited guidance given the unprecedented demand environment.

BULL CASE - $1,400 TARGET (+52%)

The bull case assumes AI infrastructure spending accelerates beyond current forecasts as enterprise adoption broadens. Revenue reaches $150 billion in FY2026 (+45% YoY) with data center growing 55%. Gross margins expand to 78% from operating leverage. Multiple of 40x forward P/E on premium growth.

BASE CASE - $1,200 TARGET (+30%)

Base case assumes AI demand remains strong but normalizes from peak growth rates. Revenue of $125 billion (+25% YoY) with data center maintaining 80%+ share. Competition from AMD and custom chips pressures margins modestly. Multiple compresses to 32x as growth decelerates.

BEAR CASE - $600 TARGET (-35%)

Bear case models AI spending disappointment as enterprises struggle to monetize investments. Revenue of $95 billion (+5% YoY) as hyperscaler orders slow. Gross margins compress to 68% from competitive pressure and inventory buildup. Multiple de-rates to 22x reflecting normalized growth.

RECOMMENDATION

We rate Nvidia Outperform with a $1,200 target reflecting the base case. The bull case has meaningful probability given early stages of AI adoption, while bear case risks appear contained by current order backlog.
