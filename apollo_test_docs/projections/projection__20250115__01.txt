Title: US Equity Market Outlook 2025
Date: 2025-01-15
Category: projection
Source: Morgan Stanley Research

MARKET FORECAST

We project the S&P 500 to reach 5,400-5,600 by year-end 2025, representing potential upside of 8-12% from current levels. Our base case assumes continued economic expansion with moderating inflation allowing the Federal Reserve to begin rate cuts in mid-2025.

EARNINGS PROJECTIONS

S&P 500 earnings per share are expected to grow 9% in 2025 to $265, following estimated growth of 8% in 2024. Technology and healthcare sectors are projected to lead with double-digit earnings growth, while energy and materials face headwinds from commodity price normalization.

SECTOR RECOMMENDATIONS

Overweight: Technology, Healthcare, Industrials
Market Weight: Financials, Consumer Discretionary
Underweight: Energy, Utilities, Real Estate

Technology benefits from AI infrastructure spending and cloud adoption. Healthcare offers defensive characteristics with growth from new drug approvals. Industrials gain from reshoring trends and infrastructure investment.

RISK FACTORS

Key risks include persistent inflation requiring extended tight monetary policy, geopolitical tensions affecting global trade, and potential earnings disappointments if consumer spending weakens. A recession scenario would imply 15-20% downside to current market levels.

VALUATION ASSESSMENT

The market trades at 21x forward earnings, above the 10-year average of 18x but supported by lower interest rate expectations and strong corporate fundamentals. We view current valuations as fair given the earnings growth outlook.
