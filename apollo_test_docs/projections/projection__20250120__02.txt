Title: Inflation and Interest Rate Forecast Q1 2025
Date: 2025-01-20
Category: projection
Source: Federal Reserve Bank Economic Research

INFLATION OUTLOOK

Core PCE inflation is projected to decline from 2.8% in December 2024 to 2.4% by June 2025 and reach the Federal Reserve's 2% target by early 2026. Shelter costs remain the primary driver of above-target inflation but are expected to moderate as the lagged effect of slower rent growth flows through.

INTEREST RATE PROJECTIONS

We anticipate the Federal Open Market Committee will begin reducing the federal funds rate in June 2025, with three 25 basis point cuts bringing the target range to 4.50-4.75% by year-end. Further cuts in 2026 would bring rates to a neutral level of approximately 3.00%.

YIELD CURVE FORECAST

The 10-year Treasury yield is expected to trade in a range of 3.75-4.25% during 2025, with the curve gradually steepening as short-term rates decline more than long-term rates. The 2s-10s spread should move from negative 20 basis points to positive 50 basis points by year-end.

LABOR MARKET EXPECTATIONS

Unemployment is projected to rise modestly from 4.0% to 4.3% as labor market rebalancing continues. Job growth should average 150,000 per month, down from 200,000 in 2024 but consistent with stable economic expansion.

CONSUMER SPENDING

Real consumer spending growth is forecast at 2.2% for 2025, supported by real wage gains as inflation declines faster than nominal wage growth. Savings rates should stabilize around 4% after declining from pandemic highs.
