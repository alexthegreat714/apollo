Title: Retirement Planning Projections - Long-Term Capital Markets
Date: 2025-02-15
Category: projection
Source: Vanguard Investment Strategy Group

10-YEAR RETURN ASSUMPTIONS

US Equities: 4.5-6.5% annualized
International Developed Equities: 6.0-8.0% annualized
Emerging Market Equities: 5.5-7.5% annualized
US Aggregate Bonds: 4.0-5.0% annualized
International Bonds (Hedged): 3.5-4.5% annualized
Cash: 3.0-4.0% annualized

EXPECTED INFLATION

Long-term inflation is projected at 2.0-2.5% annually, consistent with Federal Reserve targets. This implies real return expectations of 2-4% for equities and 1.5-2.5% for bonds over the coming decade.

PORTFOLIO IMPLICATIONS

A traditional 60/40 portfolio is expected to deliver nominal returns of 5.0-6.5% annually over the next decade, below historical averages of 7-8%. Investors should consider extending time horizons or increasing savings rates to meet retirement goals.

RETIREMENT INCOME PLANNING

Safe withdrawal rates are projected at 3.5-4.0% for 30-year retirement periods, down from historical 4% guideline due to lower expected returns. Flexible withdrawal strategies that adjust for market conditions can support higher initial withdrawals.

RISK CONSIDERATIONS

Sequence of returns risk remains a primary concern for retirees. Those within 5 years of retirement should consider reducing equity exposure to minimize impact of potential market downturns on portfolio sustainability.

SOCIAL SECURITY ASSUMPTIONS

Projections assume current benefit levels continue through 2033. After Trust Fund depletion, benefits may be reduced to 77% of scheduled amounts absent legislative action. Conservative planning should incorporate potential benefit reductions.
