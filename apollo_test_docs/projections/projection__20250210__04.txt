Title: Corporate Bond Market Forecast 2025
Date: 2025-02-10
Category: projection
Source: BlackRock Fixed Income Research

CREDIT SPREAD OUTLOOK

Investment grade credit spreads are projected to remain stable at 90-110 basis points over Treasuries during 2025. Strong corporate fundamentals and moderate supply should support valuations despite tight absolute spread levels. We see limited scope for further tightening.

HIGH YIELD PROJECTIONS

High yield spreads are forecast to trade in a range of 350-450 basis points. Default rates should rise modestly from 2.5% to 3.5%, remaining below historical averages. CCC-rated credits face elevated refinancing risk as 2025 maturities approach.

RETURN EXPECTATIONS

Investment grade bonds should deliver total returns of 5-6% in 2025, primarily from carry with modest price appreciation if rates decline as expected. High yield could return 7-9% with higher income offsetting potential spread widening and defaults.

SECTOR PREFERENCES

Favored sectors include banking (strong capital positions), technology (solid balance sheets), and utilities (regulated returns). We are cautious on commercial real estate (office weakness) and healthcare (reimbursement pressures).

DURATION POSITIONING

We recommend modest duration extension given our view that interest rates will decline in the second half of 2025. Intermediate maturities (5-7 years) offer attractive risk-adjusted returns compared to longer duration exposure.

EMERGING MARKET DEBT

EM sovereign debt offers attractive yields with selective opportunities in countries with improving fundamentals. Local currency bonds benefit from potential dollar weakness, while hard currency provides higher carry with lower volatility.
