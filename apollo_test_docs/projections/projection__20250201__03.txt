Title: Global Economic Growth Projections 2025-2026
Date: 2025-02-01
Category: projection
Source: International Monetary Fund

WORLD ECONOMY OVERVIEW

Global GDP growth is projected at 3.2% for 2025 and 3.3% for 2026, reflecting steady expansion despite geopolitical uncertainties and the transition away from tight monetary policies. Advanced economies are expected to grow 1.8% while emerging markets should expand 4.3%.

UNITED STATES

US GDP growth is forecast at 2.4% for 2025, moderating from an estimated 2.8% in 2024. Consumer spending remains the primary growth driver, supported by a healthy labor market and declining inflation. Business investment should recover as financing conditions ease.

EURO AREA

The euro area is projected to grow 1.2% in 2025, improving from 0.8% in 2024. Germany shows signs of recovery in manufacturing, while France and Spain benefit from services expansion. Inflation should return to target by mid-2025, allowing the ECB to continue rate cuts.

CHINA

Chinese growth is forecast at 4.5% for 2025, supported by policy stimulus measures. The property sector remains a drag on activity, but infrastructure investment and export growth provide offsets. Consumer confidence recovery remains gradual.

EMERGING MARKETS

India leads emerging market growth at 6.5%, driven by strong domestic demand and manufacturing expansion. Brazil and Mexico benefit from nearshoring trends, while Southeast Asian economies see sustained export growth to developed markets.

RISKS TO OUTLOOK

Downside risks include escalation of regional conflicts, renewed inflation pressures from commodity shocks, and financial stress from high debt levels. Upside potential comes from faster-than-expected productivity gains from AI adoption.
